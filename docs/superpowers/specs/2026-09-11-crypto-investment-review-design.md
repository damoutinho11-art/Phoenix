# Crypto investment review contract

Separate data verification from investment judgment. A fetched price or clean
internal checks cannot alone create BUY_CANDIDATE. Conversely, research synthesis
must not erase a substantive external review by unconditionally assigning WATCH.

Introduce a versioned, dated review inside the existing memo validation JSON.
Require an exact crypto identity, long-term spot-contribution scope, thesis,
multiple risks, explicit alternatives including cash, invalidation conditions,
and dated protocol, broker/cost and market-risk sources. Require HTTPS public
source URLs and content/evidence hashes, a reviewer explicitly identified as
assistant research (not human approval), and an expiry no more than seven days
after review. Two clean validation records must bind the exact review digest:
external-source verification and investment-thesis review. These are auditable
review assertions, not proof of future returns or independent analyst consensus.

Synthesis preserves a current bound review only after existing FAIL/WARNING gates.
Expired or invalid reviews cannot yield a buy. Selection requires this review for
crypto BUY_CANDIDATE and derives freshness from the review itself, not the time an
internal quality gate ran. Existing market, cash, sizing and manual execution
checks still apply. No new panels, automated orders or optimizer promotion.

Complete a current BTC/ETH/SOL external comparison using primary protocol and
broker sources plus reproducible completed-close risk measures. Any resulting
review must distinguish strategic contribution suitability from a return forecast,
disclose the limited universe and the existing mandate, and explain whether cash
or another asset is preferable. HYPE/TAO remain outside this bounded comparison.

Verification: missing/expired/mismatched reviews, digest binding, source dates,
failed checks, synthesis preservation and selection freshness; existing research,
finance and auth regressions; independent review; deploy and verify owner memos.
