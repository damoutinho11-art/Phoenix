# Recurring contributions and cash reconciliation

A recurring BTC share is now separate from the combined crypto exposure ceiling. Version 2 owner policy supports a dated contribution start, with BTC-only new crypto contributions. Applied nonvoid EUR buy outlays (principal plus fees) determine the cumulative share shortfall. Current verified cash, exposure limits, evidence and the minimum acquired principal after fees still govern each proposal. Repeated reads and missed recommendations create no credit or cash. Unknown ledger assets or invalid history pause recommendations.

Recorded nonvoid buys on or after the effective statement date pause cash authorization until a strictly later verified statement covers the purchase. This includes manually recorded but unapplied purchases; no speculative cash deduction is made. Historical investment-capacity requests are rejected and closed weeks cannot reopen an actionable budget.

Owned fund share classes remain unverified. Recommendation output discloses assumed versus owner-recorded symbols and explicitly unavailable exact issuer overlap. Broker holdings evidence plus dated constituents are still required; this release does not establish exact overlap or optimal investment performance.
